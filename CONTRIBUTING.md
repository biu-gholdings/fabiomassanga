# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in this public repository.

## Purpose

This project supports the public website **fabiomassanga.com** and transparent founder positioning. Contributions should improve public clarity, accessibility, or documentation hygiene — not expose private operations.

## What we accept

- Typographical or accessibility improvements to public pages  
- Corrections to public links, metadata, or repository documentation  
- Security hygiene suggestions that do not require publishing sensitive detail  

## What we do not accept

- Confidential business plans, investor materials, or legal drafts  
- Infrastructure diagrams, deployment secrets, or production configuration  
- Validator logic, treasury mechanics, liquidity internals, or backend orchestration detail  
- Credentials, keys, or personal data of third parties  
- Hype, promotional spam, or unrelated marketing  

## How to contribute

1. Fork the repository (if enabled) or propose changes through your organization's process.  
2. Keep pull requests **small and focused** on public-facing scope.  
3. Do not include secrets or internal documents in commits.  
4. Use clear commit messages describing the public benefit of the change.

## Review standards

Maintainers may decline changes that:

- Expand technical surface area beyond public institutional messaging  
- Introduce operational or security-sensitive implementation detail  
- Conflict with the transparency and security posture described in [README.md](README.md) and [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)

## Questions

For general or security-related questions: **support@biu-gholdings.org**
