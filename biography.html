Biography

Fábio G. Massanga is an Angolan entrepreneur, infrastructure architect, and financial systems builder. He is the Founder of CubeShackles and Managing Member of Biu-g Holdings LLC, focused on building sovereign financial infrastructure for Africa, beginning with Angola.

He was born and raised in Cabinda, Angola, in a middle-class household, where early exposure to the country's telecommunications and banking sectors shaped his understanding of how financial access is structured-and where it breaks. Despite visible progress in Angola's financial and telecom industries, he identified persistent infrastructure gaps across identity verification, payment visibility, and institutional coordination that no existing system was addressing.

Massanga attended Dom Paulino Madeca School, where he distinguished himself academically and athletically, winning a junior tennis competition sponsored by Chevron-demonstrating early discipline and competitive focus. He later graduated from Instituto Privado Colegio Padre Builu with a concentration in Accounting and Management, laying the foundation for his analytical approach to business and finance.

He pursued higher education in Luanda at two institutions simultaneously: Universidade Lusiadas de Angola, where he studied Foreign Affairs, and Faculdade de Administracao & Negocios, where he focused on Marketing and Management. During this demanding period, he worked full-time in the service sector in Benfica, Luanda, financing his own education and developing the resilience that would define his entrepreneurial path.

He ultimately left university and relocated to the United States, where he began developing a deeper understanding of labor markets, economic systems, capital formation, and entrepreneurship through direct exposure and practical experience in one of the world's most competitive business environments.

His current work centers on CubeShackles-an Angola-first financial infrastructure layer designed to enable identity-linked transactions, payment visibility, informal market digitization, and regulator-readable operating systems. CubeShackles is being designed in alignment with Angola's central financial and regulatory institutions, including the Banco Nacional de Angola (BNA), Ministerio das Financas, Administracao Geral Tributaria (AGT), Agencia Angolana de Regulacao e Supervisao de Seguros (ARSEG), and the Comissao do Mercado de Capitais (CMC).

His thesis is that Africa's financial access challenge is not solely a banking issue, but a structural infrastructure problem requiring coordinated systems across identity, compliance, payments, and public trust. CubeShackles addresses this by building the connective layer that institutions, businesses, and citizens can rely on - regulator-readable, identity-anchored, and designed for Angola's operational reality from day one.

In parallel, Massanga founded the BIU.G Academy, a financial literacy and sovereign economic empowerment platform serving Angola and the broader African continent - providing the educational foundation that complements his infrastructure work.

He posts his views on LinkedIn and his work is documented at CubeShackles.com and BIUGAcademy.org.
