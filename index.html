Biography

FÁBIO G. MASSANGA is the Founder of CubeShackles and Managing Member of Biu-g Holdings LLC. His work focuses on building sovereign financial infrastructure for Africa, beginning with Angola.

He was raised in Cabinda, Angola, in a middle-class household, where early exposure to telecommunications and banking systems shaped his understanding of how financial access is structured—and where it breaks. Despite visible progress in Angola’s financial and telecom sectors, he identified persistent infrastructure gaps across identity, payments, and institutional coordination.

Massanga attended Dom Paulino Madeca School, where he won a junior tennis competition sponsored by Chevron, demonstrating early discipline and competitive focus. He later graduated from Instituto Privado Colégio Padre Builu with a concentration in Accounting and Management.

He pursued higher education in Luanda across two institutions: Universidade Lusíadas de Angola (Foreign Affairs) and Faculdade de Administração & Negócios (Marketing & Management). During this period, he worked full-time in the service sector in Benfica, Luanda, supporting himself while continuing his academic path.

He ultimately left university and relocated to the United States, where he began developing a deeper understanding of labor markets, economic systems, and entrepreneurship through direct exposure and practical experience.

His current work centers on CubeShackles—an Angola-first infrastructure layer designed to enable identity-linked transactions, payment visibility, informal market digitization, and regulator-readable financial systems. His thesis is that Africa’s financial access challenge is not solely a banking issue, but a structural infrastructure problem requiring coordinated systems across identity, compliance, payments, and public trust.
