Biography

Fábio G. Massanga is the founder and CEO of CubeShackles, focused on identity, payments, compliance, and transaction visibility.

Why Angola First

Angola is the right first market because the infrastructure gap is visible and measurable.

Informal markets dominate economic activity but lack structured financial infrastructure.

CubeShackles begins in Angola to deploy a clear wedge: identity-linked, phone-based transactions with institutional visibility.
