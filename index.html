Biography

Fábio Massanga is the founder and CEO of CubeShackles, an Angola-first financial infrastructure company focused on identity, payments, compliance, and transaction visibility.

His work is centered on the belief that Africa does not have a financial access problem alone — it has an infrastructure alignment problem.

CubeShackles is being built from local economic reality: informal markets, fragmented identity systems, underdeveloped financial rails, and the need for infrastructure that can be read by institutions and regulators.

CubeCoin

CubeCoin is the proposed native economic layer for CubeShackles.

It is designed around validation, infrastructure access, and ecosystem participation — not as a speculative promise.

Its role is to support the economic coordination of identity-linked transactions, network participation, and regulated infrastructure usage.
