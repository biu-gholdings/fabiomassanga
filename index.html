Current Focus

I am building CubeShackles: an Angola-first financial infrastructure system designed for identity, payments, compliance, informal market digitization, and institutional-grade transaction visibility.

Thesis

Africa’s financial systems are not broken — they are incomplete. Most infrastructure was not designed for informal markets, fragmented identity systems, or local currency velocity.

The next generation of financial infrastructure will not be imported. It will be built natively, aligned with regulators, institutions, and real user behavior from the ground up.

CubeShackles is designed as that system — starting in Angola, where the gap between informal and formal economies creates the strongest wedge for transformation.

System Design

The system integrates identity, payments, compliance, and transaction visibility into a unified financial layer.

Built on a distributed structure of cubes and shackles, it enables real-time transaction flow, delayed settlement, and institutional-grade auditability across regions.

Infrastructure
