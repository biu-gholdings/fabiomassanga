Fábio G. Massanga

Fábio G. Massanga is the Founder of CubeShackles and Managing Member of Biu-g Holdings LLC. His work focuses on building sovereign financial infrastructure for Africa, beginning with Angola.

He was raised in Cabinda, Angola, where early exposure to telecommunications and banking systems shaped his understanding of how financial access is structured and where it breaks. He identified persistent infrastructure gaps across identity, payments, and institutional coordination.

Massanga attended Dom Paulino Madeca School and graduated from Instituto Privado Colégio Padre Builu with a concentration in Accounting and Management. He pursued higher education at Universidade Lusíadas de Angola and Faculdade de Administração & Negócios while working full-time in Benfica, Luanda.

He relocated to the United States, where he developed a deeper understanding of labor markets, economic systems, and entrepreneurship. His current work centers on CubeShackles, an Angola-first infrastructure layer for identity-linked transactions, payment visibility, and regulator-readable financial systems.
