Building Sovereign Financial
Infrastructure for Angola

Institutional-grade financial infrastructure focused on identity-linked transactions, payment visibility, and regulator-readable operating systems. Fábio G. Massanga is the founder of CubeShackles and CubeCoin, and the creator of the BIU.G Academy — a platform dedicated to building financial literacy and sovereign economic empowerment across Angola and the African continent.

Angola First

Deployment strategy begins in Angola through a controlled pilot framework, with measurable institutional alignment before regional expansion.

Regulatory & Institutional Alignment

CubeShackles is being designed in alignment with Angola's financial, tax, and regulatory institutions to ensure transparency, auditability, and national compliance from inception.
