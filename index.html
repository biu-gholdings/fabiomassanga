Angola’s Financial Infrastructure Layer —
Built from First Principles

Identity. Payments. Compliance. Unified into a single, regulator-readable system designed to operate across Angola’s formal and informal economy.

The System

A unified infrastructure layer designed to coordinate identity, transactions, and regulatory visibility at the national level.

Identity Layer

National identity-linked transaction system enabling verified participation across all financial interactions.

Payment Layer

Unified transaction rails supporting tokenized value, settlement systems, and real-time financial flows across markets.

Compliance Layer

Built-in auditability and regulator-readable infrastructure enabling real-time oversight and institutional alignment.

Angola First

Deployment begins in Angola through controlled institutional pilots, with measurable regulatory alignment before regional expansion.

Regulatory & Institutional Alignment

Engineered to operate within Angola’s financial and regulatory architecture from inception. Regulators are not external observers — they are system participants.
